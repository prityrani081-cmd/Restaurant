Grilled Chicken & Fries

Juicy grilled chicken served with crispy golden fries and grilled vegetables.

About This Dish

To prepare juicy and flavorful grilled chicken, pound the meat to an even half-inch thickness, marinate it for at least 30 minutes, and grill it over medium-high heat for 5 to 7 minutes per side until the internal temperature reaches 165°F

ingredients

a base of chicken (breasts or thighs), fat or oil for moisture, and a blend of seasonings or a marinade made from acid, salt, and aromatics
